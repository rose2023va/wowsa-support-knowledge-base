# Paid advertising placement requests

## What it is

Requests to place paid advertising on WOWSA's website, newsletter, or other channels.

## Where to check

`SUPPORT/Advertising` label for prior precedent and any standard rate/placement info that's been sent before.

## How to answer

Acknowledge and confirm it's being routed. Don't quote pricing unless there's a documented current rate card to point to.

## Escalate if

Always, unless a current, confirmed rate card exists to reference directly.

# Sponsorship proposals

## What it is

A company or brand proposing to sponsor a WOWSA event, program, or the organization generally.

## Where to check

No confirmed single source of truth for active sponsorship pipeline as of this writing (see folder README). Check `SUPPORT/Partnership/Affiliations` for prior similar threads to see how they were historically handled.

## How to answer

Acknowledge and confirm the proposal was received. Don't commit WOWSA to any sponsorship terms yourself.

## Escalate if

Always, for anything beyond acknowledgment. Sponsorship decisions need Rose/Quinn.

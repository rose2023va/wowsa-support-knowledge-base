# General partnership/collaboration proposals

## What it is

Organizations or individuals proposing some form of collaboration that isn't specifically sponsorship or advertising (content partnerships, co-branded programs, data/research collaborations).

## Where to check

`SUPPORT/Partnership/Affiliations` label for precedent. If it overlaps with an existing WOWSA initiative (education, ratification-as-a-product for associations, Safer Together), route to whoever owns that specific initiative rather than treating it as generic business development.

## How to answer

Acknowledge, ask clarifying questions if the proposal is vague, and confirm it's being routed for review.

## Escalate if

Always, for any actual decision. This category exists to triage and route, not to negotiate.

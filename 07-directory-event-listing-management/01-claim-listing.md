# Claiming an existing listing

## What it is

Someone found a listing about themselves or their organization already on WOWSA's Directory and wants to claim ownership/editing rights.

## Where to check

`openwaterswimming.com/docs/` → "Claim Your Listing" and "Find Your Listing" articles are the maintained step-by-step process, link directly rather than re-explaining. Check the live Directory to confirm the listing exists and its current claim status before answering.

## How to answer

Walk them through the docs article. If they're having trouble finding their listing, help search the Directory for likely name/organization matches.

## Escalate if

The claim flow itself appears broken (a `10-technical-website-issues/` situation), or two people are disputing the same listing.

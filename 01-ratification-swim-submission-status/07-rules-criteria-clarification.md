# Rules/criteria clarification (general)

## What it is

General questions about ratification rules that aren't tied to a specific swim in progress: what counts as a marathon swim, wetsuit rules, relay vs solo criteria, etc.

## How to recognize it

Hypothetical or general-knowledge phrasing: "does WOWSA ratify wetsuit swims," "what's the minimum distance," "how does relay ratification work."

## Where to check

`openwaterswimming.com/docs/` → "Rules & Regulations" category (Marathon Swimming Rules & Regulations, Racing Rules & Regulations, WOWSA Code of Conduct) and "Independent Marathon Swim Ratification" category. These are the maintained, public, canonical answer, link to them rather than restating rules from memory since they can change.

## How to answer

- Link the specific docs article, then give a short direct answer to their specific question pulled from that article.
- If their question spans multiple articles, synthesize briefly but still link the sources.

## Escalate if

The question exposes a genuine ambiguity in the published rules (not just the customer not having read them) — flag for the docs to be clarified, don't invent a ruling.

# Pre-approval / pre-swim procedure inquiry

## What it is

A swimmer or organizer planning an upcoming swim, asking what they need to do *before* attempting it for the swim to be eligible for ratification (GPS device requirements, observer requirements, route documentation, notification deadlines). Currently answered fresh/long-form each time by whoever picks up the ticket, no standard canned answer exists yet.

## How to recognize it

Forward-looking language: "planning to swim," "want to make sure this qualifies," "what do I need before I start." Often includes a proposed route, date, and questions about tracker/GPS requirements.

## Where to check

- `openwaterswimming.com/docs/` → "Independent Marathon Swim Ratification" category has the GPS device guidance, observer role documentation, and documentation standards articles. Link directly to the relevant article rather than re-typing the requirements from memory, requirements can change and the docs page is the maintained source.
- `openwaterswimming.com/contact-wowsa/` → "WOWSA Observer Log Template" for what the observer needs to fill out during the swim.
- If the question is genuinely novel (a route type or circumstance the docs don't cover), don't improvise official rules. Escalate.

## How to answer

- Lead with the docs article link, then summarize the 2-3 most relevant points for their specific situation rather than pasting the whole article.
- Confirm they know an Independent Observer is required (see `03-independent-observer-paperwork.md` if they ask who qualifies).
- If pre-approval requires a Jotform submission ahead of the swim, point them to it and note the typical review turnaround if known.

## Escalate if

- The proposed route or circumstance isn't clearly covered by the existing docs (e.g. unusual water body, relay structure, safety-conflict situation).
- They're asking about eligibility for an already-completed swim that wasn't pre-approved, that's a different, more sensitive conversation, don't promise retroactive ratification.

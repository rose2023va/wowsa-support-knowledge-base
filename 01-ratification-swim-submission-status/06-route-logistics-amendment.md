# Route/logistics amendment mid-swim

## What it is

A swimmer or organizer reporting a change made during or shortly before the swim: start point moved, tracker/GPS device swapped mid-swim, route adjusted for weather or safety. A real recurring pattern, not something anticipated in the original ratification design, so there's no fully standard process yet.

## How to recognize it

"We had to change the start point because...," "our tracker died and we swapped to...," "route was adjusted due to conditions."

## Where to check

- **Ratifications Pipeline** (`appgUjmgd0K8WWp31`): check if the amendment is already logged in Notes.
- Google Drive admin/ops folder has a "Ratifications" subfolder with route-approval reference materials (e.g. past route-approval screenshots) that can help compare the amended route against what was originally approved.

## How to answer

- Acknowledge the amendment and confirm it's noted on file.
- Don't independently judge whether the amendment affects ratification eligibility, that's a reviewer call.
- Ask for whatever documentation supports the amendment (why the change happened, replacement tracker data, etc) if not already provided.

## Escalate if

- The amendment is significant enough that it might affect route-distance calculations or eligibility (this is genuinely a judgment call every time, always loop in whoever does route/distance review).

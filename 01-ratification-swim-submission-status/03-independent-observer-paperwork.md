# Independent Observer paperwork/rules

## What it is

Questions about who can serve as an Independent Observer, what paperwork they need to file (Observer Agreement Forms, Change of Observer requests), and how observer documentation feeds into ratification.

## Known gap, be upfront about it

**There is currently no official approved-observer list.** This is a real, unresolved policy gap, not something you can paper over with a confident answer. Agents are effectively answering a moving target here. If a customer asks "is [person] an approved observer," there is no master list to check against — evaluate based on the Observer Agreement Form and documentation standards, and when genuinely unsure, say so rather than asserting an answer that doesn't exist yet.

## How to recognize it

"Who can be my observer," "does my observer need to be certified," "changing observer," "Observer Agreement Form questions."

## Where to check

- `openwaterswimming.com/contact-wowsa/` → "WOWSA Observer Log Template."
- `openwaterswimming.com/docs/` → "Independent Marathon Swim Ratification" category for the observer-role article.
- For a specific swim's observer status, check the Swim ID's record in **Ratifications Pipeline** (`appgUjmgd0K8WWp31`).

## How to answer

- Explain what the Observer Log Template requires and point to it directly.
- For "who qualifies" questions, answer based on the documented general criteria (independence from the swimmer, ability to log the swim accurately), not a specific approved list, since none exists.
- For Change of Observer requests, confirm the swim's current status first (if already ratified, this is a different, more sensitive process than if it's still pending).

## Escalate if

- Customer is asking to challenge or dispute an observer determination already made.
- The situation involves a genuinely ambiguous observer-independence question (e.g. observer has a personal/business relationship with the swimmer) — this needs judgment, not a scripted answer.

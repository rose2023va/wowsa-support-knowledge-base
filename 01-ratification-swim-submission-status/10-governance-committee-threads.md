# Governance/committee threads

## What it is

Internal-facing correspondence about the ratification review committee itself (who's reviewing, committee assignment, committee process questions) rather than a customer asking about their own swim.

## Scope note

This is mostly internal-facing and likely out of scope for a customer support playbook. If a ticket in this bucket is genuinely from a committee member or reviewer (not an external customer), it's probably not something a new support hire should be drafting replies for at all.

## How to answer

Don't draft a reply. Confirm the sender is internal/committee, and route directly to Rose or whoever manages the review committee.

## Escalate if

Always route to the person managing ratification committee operations. Not a general support queue item.

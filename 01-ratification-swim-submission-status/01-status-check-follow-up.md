# Status check / progress follow-up

## What it is

A swimmer, coach, or association contact asking where their submitted swim stands in the ratification process. Often a repeat/"gentle reminder" follow-up because the first inquiry went unanswered. This is the single highest-volume, highest-leverage sub-issue in the whole knowledge base.

## How to recognize it

Subject lines like "Re: [Swim name] Ratification," "Following up on ratification status," "Any update on..." Sender is usually the swimmer directly, a support crew member, or (for partner-association swims) the association contact. Often already has a `SUPPORT/Ratifications/SWIM-####` label if it's a repeat thread.

## Where to check

1. If the thread has a `SUPPORT/Ratifications/SWIM-####` label, that's the Swim ID — search for it directly in **Ratifications Pipeline** (`appgUjmgd0K8WWp31`), table "Workflow," field "Swim ID."
2. If no Swim ID is visible, search Workflow by Swimmer's Name.
3. Check the "Status" and "Notes" fields. Status will show where it sits (submitted, under review, awaiting evidence, ratified, etc).
4. **First, check whether this is even a standard-pipeline swim.** If the sender is a partner association, or the thread references the "Pre-approved Ratification" form or names an association (LPSA, Swim Oresund, OWAAF India), it is a **pre-approved ratification**, a different process. It will NOT be in the Workflow table and that is normal, not a lost submission. Stop here and go to `11-pre-approved-ratification-partner-association.md`.

## How to answer

- Give a real, specific status from the Workflow record, not a generic "we're reviewing it."
- If it's genuinely stuck (older than a few weeks with no movement) and you can see why (missing evidence, awaiting observer confirmation, etc), say what's actually needed to move it forward.
- If you cannot tell why it's stuck from the Notes field, do not guess. Say you're checking with the team and will follow up with a real answer, then flag it to Rose rather than sending a vague reply.
- This category has a known pattern of some cases (particularly OWAAF India submissions, per past review) going months with only repeated unanswered "gentle reminders." If a thread shows more than one prior unanswered follow-up, treat it as priority, not routine.

## Escalate if

- No record exists in Workflow at all for a swim the customer says they submitted. **Before treating this as a lost submission, rule out that it is a pre-approved ratification** (see step 1 above), which legitimately does not appear in that table. If it is genuinely a standard-pipeline swim with no record, don't tell the customer it's missing, check with Rose first.
- The case has been open more than 60 days with no visible movement in Notes.
- The customer's tone indicates frustration or an implied complaint about the delay itself — that can tip into `11-next-in-line/complaints-disputes-legal.md` territory.

# Dispute/correction of a ratification decision

## What it is

A customer disagreeing with a ratification outcome (denied, or ratified with conditions they dispute) and asking for it to be reconsidered or corrected.

## This is escalate-only

Do not draft a customer-facing reply defending, reversing, or explaining a contested ratification decision. This needs a human with actual decision-making authority.

## Where to check

At the association level (partner-association-administered ratifications), a formal Dispute Resolution Protocol document already exists, check with Rose/Quinn for the current version rather than assuming WOWSA's own process mirrors it exactly.

## How to answer

Acknowledge receipt, confirm it's being escalated for review, and give a realistic sense of next steps (who will respond, roughly when) if known. Do not speculate about the outcome.

## Escalate if

Always. This entire sub-issue is an escalation, not a draft-a-reply situation. Route to Rose/Quinn immediately.

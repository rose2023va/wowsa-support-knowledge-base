# Record/first-claim inquiries

## What it is

A swimmer or their team asserting their swim sets a record (first person/fastest/oldest/etc to complete a route) and asking WOWSA to recognize or document that.

## Important: this is not a scriptable case

Record and first-claim inquiries require individual judgment about route legitimacy and historical precedent. Do not draft a definitive answer confirming or denying a record claim.

## How to recognize it

"This would be the first/fastest," "record claim," "did anyone swim this before."

## Where to check

- **Ratifications Pipeline** (`appgUjmgd0K8WWp31`) for the swim's own ratification status, that has to be settled before a record claim is even evaluated.
- **WOWSA Awards Archive** (`appQIADxmbGpN8PbN`) — table "Award Records" and "Entities" — for historical precedent on similar claims, though this base is primarily built for the WOWSA Awards program, not a formal swim-records registry. Treat it as a research aid, not the final word.
- **Open Water Swim Database** (`appt2yM6lelJKt9Yx`) and **Swim Database** (`appTNkrTG5GYOgqgH`, marked favorite, likely most actively used) may hold broader historical swim records for cross-reference.

## How to answer

- Confirm the swim's ratification status is settled first, that's a prerequisite.
- Acknowledge the record claim was received and is being reviewed. Do not confirm or deny the claim yourself.
- Route the actual determination to whoever handles records/awards editorial review.

## Escalate if

Always escalate the actual determination. Your job here is acknowledgment and status only, not the ruling.

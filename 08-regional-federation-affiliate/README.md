# 08 — Regional Federation/Affiliate Correspondence

**Rank: ~7% of sampled inbound, medium reply-need.**

## What this category is

Ongoing correspondence with existing partner associations and regional federations (distinct from `04-partnership-sponsorship-marketing/`, which covers *new* business-development outreach). WOWSA co-ratifies swims with partner associations under a "pre-approved" workflow, and some associations (e.g. **LPSA**) submit swims on behalf of their members via the same Jotform pre-approval process used for direct submissions.

**Gmail label**: `SUPPORT/LPSA` (confirmed real, seen in production — the "Name of Association/Partner" field on the Pre-Approved Ratification Jotform). Other regional/federation threads will mostly show up in `SUPPORT/Ratifications` or `SUPPORT/Pre-Approved Ratifications` without their own dedicated per-association label, LPSA is simply the one that got its own label historically. Watch for other association names appearing in the same Jotform field, they're the same pattern even without a dedicated label.

**Public reference**: `openwaterswimming.com/docs/` → "Associations & Federations" category (Oceans Seven Associations, Global Marathon Swimming: A Guide to Associations & Federations, Worldwide Swimming Federations, Guinness World Records).

## Where the strategic context lives

WOWSA is building a formal **multi-tenant ratification-as-a-product** model: each partner association eventually gets its own scoped Airtable workspace (Cases, Documentation, Observers, Review committees, Decisions, etc.) with full data isolation, licensed as infrastructure rather than the current ad hoc co-ratification arrangement. This is locked strategy, not yet built. Public wording must always read "Ratified by [Association] · Administered using WOWSA Ratification Infrastructure," never implying WOWSA co-ratified unless WOWSA is the actual ratifying body — get this exactly right in any drafted reply that references a partner association's ratification.

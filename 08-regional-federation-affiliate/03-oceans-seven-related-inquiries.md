# Oceans Seven-related questions

## What it is

Questions specifically about the Oceans Seven challenge (a recognized set of seven channel swims) and its associations.

## Where to check

`openwaterswimming.com/docs/` → "Associations & Federations" category → "Oceans Seven Associations" article is the maintained reference.

## How to answer

Link the docs article directly, then answer their specific question from it.

## Escalate if

The question involves a specific swimmer's Oceans Seven progress/ratification status across multiple swims, that needs a cross-check against **Ratifications Pipeline** (`appgUjmgd0K8WWp31`) for each individual swim, don't summarize progress from memory.

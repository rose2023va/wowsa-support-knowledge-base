# Complaints, disputes & legal-adjacent requests

**Escalate-only. Never draft a customer-facing reply for anything in this category, regardless of how confident you feel about the facts.**

## What it is

Complaints (about WOWSA, a coach, an event, another swimmer), formal disputes, defamation/takedown requests, athlete-safety allegations, or anything else with legal exposure or real reputational risk. Historically low volume (~1% of sample) but the highest-stakes category in the entire knowledge base.

## How to recognize it

Tone shift toward frustration or formality, words like "complaint," "dispute," "legal," "unacceptable," "I am reporting," allegations about a specific named person's conduct, anything involving a minor or a safety incident.

## How to handle it

1. Do not draft a reply.
2. Do not attempt to resolve, apologize on WOWSA's behalf, or make any commitment.
3. Immediately flag to Rose (and Quinn if it's policy/reputation-level) with a plain factual summary of what was received.
4. If it involves an active safety or misconduct allegation naming a specific person, treat it with extra care, do not forward or discuss details beyond the people who need to handle it.

## Why this is handled differently from everything else in this repo

Every other file in this knowledge base is written to help a new agent draft a confident, accurate reply. This one exists specifically to say: don't. A wrong or premature reply here carries real legal and reputational risk that a delay doesn't.

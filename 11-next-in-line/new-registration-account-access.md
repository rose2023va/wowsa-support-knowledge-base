# New Registration / account access issues

## What it is

Two very different things share the `SUPPORT/New Registration` label family: (1) automated "Welcome to WOWSA" account-creation confirmation emails WOWSA sends out (`SUPPORT/New Registration/Welcome Emails`, `SUPPORT/New Registration/Notification/Coaches Notifications`) — high volume, purely transactional, not real contact-driver tickets, and (2) genuine customers writing in because registration or account access isn't working. Don't confuse the two when estimating volume, most of the label's traffic is #1.

## Where to check

For genuine access issues, same as `10-technical-website-issues/01-login-account-access-issues.md`: `openwaterswimming.com/docs/` → "Login and Register" category, `ewpa/wc-get-customers` to confirm the account exists.

## How to answer

Same guidance as `10-technical-website-issues/01-login-account-access-issues.md`. This file exists mainly to flag the labeling nuance above so volume estimates for genuine tickets aren't inflated by the automated welcome-email traffic sharing the same label tree.

## Escalate if

Same triggers as `10-technical-website-issues/01-login-account-access-issues.md`.

# Safer Together community program inquiries

## What it is

**Safer Together** is a WOWSA community safety initiative with its own intake form ("Safer Together Interest Form," Jotform). People sign up by role (Swimmer, Coach, Organizer, Safety Professional, Volunteer) and provide a short bio and answer a question about providing safer experiences for swimmers. This generates a very large volume of inbound Gmail traffic (hundreds of threads), almost all of it automated form-submission notifications rather than genuine support tickets, but it hadn't been captured in the original contact-driver sample and is worth knowing about.

## How to recognize it

Sender `noreply@jotform.com`, subject "Safer Together Interest Form - [Name]" or "Clone of Safer Together Interest Form - [Name]" (a duplicate/test form variant exists, "Clone of..." — if a customer says they filled out the form but nothing happened, check whether they used the clone version by mistake).

## Where to check

No dedicated Airtable tracking base confirmed for Safer Together as of this writing, submissions currently live only as Jotform notification emails in Gmail. This is a real gap, not a lookup you're missing, flag to Rose if a customer needs status beyond "we received your submission."

## How to answer

Most of these don't need an individual reply, they're intake confirmations. If someone writes in with an actual question about the program (what it involves, what happens after signing up), acknowledge what you know from the form itself and check with Rose/Quinn for anything about the program's actual structure or next steps, since this isn't documented elsewhere yet.

## Escalate if

Anyone asks a substantive question about what Safer Together actually does or what comes after signing up, this program's full documentation doesn't exist yet in this knowledge base.

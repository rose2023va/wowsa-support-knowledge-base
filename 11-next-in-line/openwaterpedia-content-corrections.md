# Openwaterpedia/content correction requests

## What it is

Requests to correct or update content on Openwaterpedia (WOWSA's knowledge/wiki-style content) or general site articles. Low volume (~1% of sample).

## Where to check

WordPress content tools: `ewpa/get-posts`, `ewpa/get-post`, `ewpa/search-replace` (for straightforward text corrections in existing content). Confirm the correction is factually accurate before making any edit.

## How to answer

Acknowledge the correction request. For simple, clearly-correct factual fixes (a typo, a wrong date that's easily verifiable), you may be able to make the edit directly if you have publish rights and confidence in the correction. For anything involving a person's identity, achievement claims, or historical accuracy, treat like `05-record-achievement-recognition/03-awards-archive-correction-request.md`, escalate rather than editing directly.

## Escalate if

The correction is about a living person's biography or achievement claims, or anything not trivially verifiable.

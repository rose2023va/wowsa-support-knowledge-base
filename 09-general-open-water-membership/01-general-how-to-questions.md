# General "how do I..." questions

## What it is

Broad questions about how WOWSA works, how open water swimming ratification/certification/membership works in general, often from someone new to WOWSA entirely.

## Where to check

`openwaterswimming.com/docs/` is built for exactly this, browse the 8 categories (Governance & Transparency, Claim Your Listing, Login and Register, Add a Listing, Rules & Regulations, Associations & Federations, Independent Marathon Swim Ratification, WOWSA Research Libraries) to find the closest match before answering from general knowledge.

## How to answer

Point to the specific docs article. If their question spans multiple categories, give a short direct answer and link the most relevant 1-2 articles rather than every possible one.

## Escalate if

The question reveals a real gap in the docs (nothing covers it), flag it as a candidate for a new docs article rather than improvising an authoritative-sounding answer.

# Account/membership status questions

## What it is

Questions about their WOWSA account itself (registration status, what membership includes, account details).

## Where to check

`openwaterswimming.com/docs/` → "Login and Register" category. For account-linked purchases/credentials, cross-check WooCommerce (`ewpa/wc-get-customers`) and, if relevant, Kajabi enrollment.

## How to answer

Confirm actual account status before answering generally. "Membership" at WOWSA isn't a single uniform product, it can mean directory listing access, course enrollment, or general site registration depending on context, clarify which one they mean if unclear.

## Escalate if

Their account seems to be in a broken/inconsistent state across systems (e.g. WooCommerce shows a customer record but no WordPress login works).

# 09 — General Open Water Swimming/Membership Questions

**Rank: ~5% of sampled inbound, medium reply-need.**

## What this category is

Broad, general questions that don't fit a more specific category: general "how does WOWSA work" questions, membership/account questions, and genuinely miscellaneous inquiries. This is the catch-all category, expect it to overlap with others, if a ticket fits a more specific folder better, use that one instead.

**Gmail label**: `SUPPORT/General Inquiries` is the main catch-all label, and is also where a lot of miscategorized tickets from other categories end up. Always double check whether a `SUPPORT/General Inquiries`-labeled thread is actually general, or is really a ratification/billing/course question that just didn't get auto-sorted.

# General refund requests (non-course)

## What it is

Refund requests for shop purchases, ratification fees, or other non-course orders. (For course-specific refunds, see `02-course-certification-education/07-refunds-cancellations.md`.)

## Where to check

`ewpa/wc-get-order` for the specific order's payment and status details.

## How to answer

Confirm order details before committing to anything. Don't promise a refund timeline or amount without checking current policy.

## Escalate if

Any refund outside a clearly documented standard policy window, or any request involving a ratification fee (those can be tied to work already done reviewing the submission, check with Rose before refunding).

# Subscription/membership renewal or cancellation

## What it is

Questions about an upcoming renewal charge, or requests to cancel a recurring subscription/membership.

## Where to check

WooCommerce Subscriptions data via `ewpa/wc-get-order`/`ewpa/wc-get-customers` (WooCommerce Subscriptions plugin is active per the site's plugin list). Confirm the specific subscription product and its renewal date/status.

## How to answer

Give the actual renewal date and amount from the order record. For cancellation requests, confirm the action taken and the effective date (does access end immediately or at the end of the current paid period).

## Escalate if

The customer disputes having ever signed up for a recurring charge, that's closer to a billing dispute than a routine cancellation, handle carefully and consider escalating.

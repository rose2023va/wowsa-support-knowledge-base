# Verifying/documenting a record claim

## What it is

A request to verify or document a specific swimming achievement or record for historical purposes, separate from an Awards nomination.

## Where to check

**WOWSA Awards Archive** (`appQIADxmbGpN8PbN`) "Entities" and "Award Records" tables for existing documentation. Also check **Ratifications Pipeline** (`appgUjmgd0K8WWp31`) if the claim is tied to a specific swim's ratification status, and `01-ratification-swim-submission-status/05-record-first-claim-inquiries.md` for the ratification-side handling of the same kind of request.

## How to answer

Acknowledge and confirm what's currently on file, if anything. Don't independently verify or deny a record claim, that's an editorial/research judgment call (see the Awards Archive's own "Research Issues" table for how WOWSA tracks these determinations internally).

## Escalate if

Always, for the actual verification determination itself.

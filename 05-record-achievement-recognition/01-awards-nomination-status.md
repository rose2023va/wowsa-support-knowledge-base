# Status of a submitted awards nomination

## What it is

Someone who submitted (or was submitted as) a WOWSA Awards nominee, asking about status.

## Where to check

**WOWSA Awards Archive** (`appQIADxmbGpN8PbN`), "Nominations" table — search by Nominee Name or Nominator Email. Cross-reference "Award Records" if the cycle has progressed to finalist/winner stage.

## How to answer

Give the actual status from the Nominations/Award Records tables. If the cycle's review process is still in progress, say so plainly rather than a vague "thank you for your patience."

## Escalate if

The nomination isn't findable in Airtable at all despite the customer having a confirmation of submission, don't assume it was lost, check with Rose first, it may be a lookup issue rather than a missing record.

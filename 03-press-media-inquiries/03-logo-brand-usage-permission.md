# Requests to use WOWSA's name/logo/content

## What it is

Someone asking permission to use WOWSA's name, logo, or published content (e.g. an association referencing WOWSA ratification in their own materials, a swimmer using WOWSA's logo on personal materials).

## Where to check

`openwaterswimming.com/docs/` → "Governance & Transparency" for any published brand/usage policy. If nothing is published, there may not be a formal policy yet.

## How to answer

Don't grant brand usage permission yourself without a documented policy to point to. Acknowledge the request.

## Escalate if

Always, unless there's a clearly documented, current usage policy to cite directly.

# 03 — Press/Media Inquiries & Partner Press Releases

**Rank: largest by raw volume (~20% of sampled inbound) but low reply-need — mostly one-way FYI, not a real "ticket" most of the time.**

## What this category is

Press releases forwarded to WOWSA by partner associations/events, media interview requests, and journalists reaching out for facts or quotes about open water swimming/WOWSA. A lot of this volume is genuinely just informational (partner orgs CC'ing or forwarding their own press releases) and doesn't need a reply at all — the job here is mostly triage: recognize what's FYI-only vs what actually needs a response.

**Gmail labels**: no dedicated label confirmed for this category specifically; check `SUPPORT/General Inquiries` and `SUPPORT/Editorial` as the closest matches, and the sender's own signature/domain (news outlets, association press contacts) is often the fastest tell.

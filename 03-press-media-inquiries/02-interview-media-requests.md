# Journalist interview or fact-check requests

## What it is

A journalist or media outlet asking for an interview, quote, or fact verification about open water swimming, a specific swim, or WOWSA itself.

## Where to check

`openwaterswimming.com/docs/` → "Governance & Transparency" category (Charter, Nonprofit Status, Bylaws) covers most factual "what is WOWSA" questions. For fact-checks about a specific swim, check **Ratifications Pipeline** (`appgUjmgd0K8WWp31`) or the **WOWSA Awards Archive** (`appQIADxmbGpN8PbN`).

## How to answer

Acknowledge the request and confirm who will actually respond (this is not something a new support hire should draft a formal quote for on their own). Provide publicly-available factual info if it's simple and already documented (e.g. WOWSA's nonprofit status), but don't speak on WOWSA's behalf for anything opinion-based or sensitive.

## Escalate if

Almost always, for anything beyond confirming already-public factual info. Route interview requests and anything reputation-sensitive to Rose/Quinn.

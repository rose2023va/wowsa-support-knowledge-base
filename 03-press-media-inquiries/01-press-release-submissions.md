# Partner/association press release forwards

## What it is

A partner association, event organizer, or swimmer's team sending WOWSA a press release about a swim, event, or achievement, usually for awareness/visibility rather than requesting a specific action.

## How to recognize it

Formal press-release formatting, often with "FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE," attached PDFs/images, sender is a comms/PR contact rather than the swimmer themselves.

## Where to check

If it relates to a ratified swim, check **Ratifications Pipeline** (`appgUjmgd0K8WWp31`) to see if the swim is already on file. If it's newsworthy WOWSA content, the WordPress blog/news section is where it could eventually be published: `ewpa/get-posts`, `ewpa/create-post` (defaults to draft).

## How to answer

Most of these need only a brief acknowledgment, not a substantive reply. If it's a strong candidate for WOWSA's own News section, flag it for editorial rather than publishing yourself.

## Escalate if

The release makes a claim (a record, an achievement) that would need verification before WOWSA republishes or comments on it, route to whoever owns editorial/awards judgment.

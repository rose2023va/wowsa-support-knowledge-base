# Course progress status

## What it is

"How much of the course have I completed," "did my progress save," "which modules do I still need."

## Where to check

Kajabi (WOWSA Academy) tracks lesson/quiz completion directly. For the credential-relevant view (which specific assessments/tags are satisfied), check the learner's record in **WOWSA Education & Credentials** (`appm0S89Eu53KWqXc`) — this is the more authoritative source for "what actually counts toward my credential" since it's built specifically to track that, not just raw lesson-view progress.

## How to answer

Give a specific module-by-module status if you can see it, not a vague "you're making progress." If they're missing only the manually-graded documentation quiz (Q4), see `01-certificate-not-received.md`, that's very likely why it looks stalled.

## Escalate if

Progress shown in Kajabi and progress shown in Airtable genuinely disagree (not just "different views of the same thing," but a real conflict) — that's a sync question for Rose.

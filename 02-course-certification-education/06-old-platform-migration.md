# References to the old WordPress/LearnDash course platform

## What it is

A customer references an old course link, login, or platform that predates the move to Kajabi's WOWSA Academy.

## Where to check

The live WordPress page `openwaterswimming.com/our-courses-are-moving-to-a-new-platform/` acknowledges this migration publicly, link them there for the official framing. LearnDash abilities (`ewpa/ld-get-courses`, `ewpa/ld-get-user-progress`) still exist on the WordPress side if you need to check whether they had progress on the old platform, but the current, correct destination for all course activity going forward is `academy.openwaterswimming.com` (Kajabi).

## How to answer

Redirect them to WOWSA Academy on Kajabi. If they had meaningful progress/purchases on the old platform, don't assume it carried over automatically, check both systems.

## Escalate if

They ask about data/progress from the old platform that isn't visible in Kajabi and you can't confirm whether or how it migrated.

# Confusion between Safe Coach and Safe Organizer Foundations

## What it is

Customer isn't sure which credential program applies to them, or purchased/enrolled in the wrong one.

## Where to check

Both programs live at `academy.openwaterswimming.com`. Safe Organizer Foundations is not a separate site, it's a route inside the same Lovable-rendered marketing pages as Safe Coach (`safecoach.openwaterswimming.com/safe-organizer`), mirroring the same structure (Hero, CHECK Framework, Foundations, Certification, Charter, FAQ, Tuition). Check the customer's actual Kajabi offer/purchase to see which one they're enrolled in.

## How to answer

- Safe Coach Foundations is for people coaching open water swimmers.
- Safe Organizer Foundations is for people organizing open water events/swims.
- If they bought the wrong one, treat as a standard course-swap/refund conversation, see `07-refunds-cancellations.md`.

## Escalate if

They need to be moved from one program's enrollment to the other and you can't do that directly in Kajabi yourself.

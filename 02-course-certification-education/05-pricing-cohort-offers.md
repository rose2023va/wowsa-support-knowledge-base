# Pricing and current cohort offers

## What it is

Questions about current price, active discounts/cohorts, or "why did my friend pay less than me."

## Where to check

Kajabi (WOWSA Academy) offers list is the live source of current pricing and which cohort offers are active. WOWSA has run time-boxed cohort launches (Advisory Board, Known Coaches, Known Organizers) with different pricing per cohort ($0 for Advisory Board, $95 early-access pricing for Known Coaches/Organizers) — these are time-limited and audience-specific, not standing discount codes, so don't assume a price a customer mentions is still valid or was ever available to everyone.

## How to answer

Quote the current live offer price from Kajabi directly rather than a remembered number, pricing has changed across cohort launches.

## Escalate if

Customer believes they qualify for a cohort-specific price (e.g. Advisory Board) and you can't confirm their eligibility, don't grant it yourself, check with Rose/Quinn.

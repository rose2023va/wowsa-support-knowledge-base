# Course refund / cancellation requests

## What it is

Customer wants a refund or to cancel a course purchase.

## Where to check

Kajabi purchase record (WOWSA Academy) for the offer and payment details. Cross-check WooCommerce (`ewpa/wc-get-orders`) if the purchase path is unclear.

## How to answer

Acknowledge the request and confirm the purchase details before processing anything. Don't promise a refund outcome or timeline you can't actually execute yourself.

## Escalate if

Any refund request past whatever WOWSA's stated refund window is (check current policy before answering, don't assume), or any case where the customer is disputing being charged at all rather than requesting a standard cancellation, that can shade into `06-billing-payment-orders/04-payment-failed-declined.md` or dispute territory.

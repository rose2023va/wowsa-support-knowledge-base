# General bug/broken-page reports

## What it is

A customer reporting something on the site isn't working as expected: broken page, error message, missing content, layout issue.

## Where to check

Try to reproduce what they're describing. `ewpa/get-page`/`ewpa/get-post`/`ewpa/get-cpt-item` to check the specific content exists and is published. `ewpa/get-active-plugins` and `ewpa/get-environment-info` if the issue looks systemic rather than content-specific.

## How to answer

Acknowledge specifically (name what's broken, don't just say "thanks for the report"), and give a realistic sense of what happens next. Don't promise a fix timeline you can't back up.

## Escalate if

Any bug that isn't a simple, obviously-fixable content issue. Don't attempt code-level or plugin-level fixes from support, that's Rose's domain.
